IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE
The relay, fan, and heater section of the proposed system involves approximately 230 V AC mains electricity. Photographs are only visual references; mains wiring should be designed and installed using properly rated enclosures, protection devices, isolation, earthing, and qualified supervision.
